\section{BCNF Check}

Consider the relations in the ERD diagram for GPU Inference Routing.

\textbf{Region}
\[
region\_id \to \{region\_name, country\}
\]

\textbf{InferenceRequest}
\[
request\_id \to \{submitted\_at, input\_size, status, region\_id, machine\_id, model\_id, response\_time\_ms\}
\]

\textbf{AIModel}
\[
model\_id \to \{model\_name\}
\]

\textbf{Hosts}
\[
(machine\_id, model\_id) \rightarrow \varnothing
\]

\textbf{GPUServer}
\[
machine\_id \to \{cpu\_capacity, memory\_capacity, gpu\_capacity, gpu\_type\_name\}
\]

\textbf{MachineMetric}
\[
metric\_id \to \{start\_time, end\_time, worker\_load, cpu\_utilization, gpu\_utilization\}
\]

\textbf{GPUType}
\[
gpu\_type\_name \to \{memory\_gb\}
\]

\textbf{CompatibleWith}
\[
(model\_id, gpu\_type\_name) \rightarrow \varnothing
\]

\bigskip
\hrule
\bigskip

All determinants are superkeys, so all relations satisfy BCNF.
